☕ Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

Overview

This project analyzes six months (January–June 2023) of point-of-sale transaction data from Bright Coffee, a multi-location coffee shop operator with three stores: Lower Manhattan, Hell's Kitchen, and Astoria. The goal was to turn ~64,000 raw transaction line items into a CEO-ready view of business performance — where revenue comes from, how it's trending, and where the company should focus next.

Dataset summary
Period covered: Jan 1, 2023 – Jun 30, 2023
Line items: 64,065
Total revenue: R698,812.33
Total units sold: 214,470
Stores: 3 (Lower Manhattan, Hell's Kitchen, Astoria)
Product categories: 9 (Coffee, Tea, Bakery, Drinking Chocolate, Coffee Beans, Branded, Loose Tea, Flavours, Packaged Chocolate)
Dayparts tracked: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night

Key findings

1. Growth trend

Revenue nearly doubled over the period — from R81.7K in January to R166.5K in June. After a February dip (-6.8%), growth accelerated sharply in March–May (+30%, +20%, +32% month-over-month), then cooled to +6% in June. All three stores grew in near-lockstep, indicating a company-wide driver rather than a single-location effect.

2. Store performance

Revenue is nearly identical across locations: Hell's Kitchen (R236.5K), Astoria (R232.2K), Lower Manhattan (R230.1K) — within 3% of each other. No underperforming or standout location.

3. Category mix

Coffee (38.6%) and Tea (28.1%) together make up two-thirds of revenue. Bakery (11.8%) and Drinking Chocolate (10.4%) follow. Packaged/retail categories (Flavours, Loose Tea, Packaged Chocolate, Branded) are each under 2% of revenue.

4. Top products

Best sellers are led by Sustainably Grown Organic Hot Chocolate (Lg), Dark Chocolate (Lg), Latte (Rg), Cappuccino (Lg), and Morning Sunrise Chai (Lg).

Main recommendations

Protect best-selling products with strong stock levels and menu visibility.
Grow Coffee and Tea sales, as they are the strongest revenue categories.
Use promotions during weaker months, particularly January and February.
Create product bundles to increase the amount customers spend per transaction.
Use store-specific strategies, especially around Astoria's stronger evening performance.
