# ☕ Bright Coffee Sales Analysis

## Transforming Coffee Sales Data into Business Insights

This project analyses Bright Coffee's sales data to identify **revenue trends, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, store performance, and opportunities for business growth**.

---

## 📌 Business Problem

Bright Coffee generates sales across multiple store locations and product categories. To support better decision-making, the raw transaction data needed to be transformed into meaningful business insights.

The analysis focuses on understanding:

* Monthly revenue performance and growth trends
* Store-level performance
* Product category and product performance
* Customer purchasing patterns by time of day
* Sales patterns by day of the week
* Revenue opportunities across different stores and product categories

The project concludes with recommendations designed to help Bright Coffee improve **sales, customer retention, operational efficiency, and average transaction value**.

## 📊 Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Monthly Sales Performance

Monthly sales were analysed to understand how Bright Coffee's revenue changed over time.

**Key finding:**
Revenue showed strong growth, particularly from March onward, with June being the strongest month in the dataset.

**Business opportunity:**
Bright Coffee should identify the activities, customer trends, or operational changes that contributed to the strong growth period and replicate successful strategies.

---

### 2. Store Performance

Sales performance was compared across the three store locations:

* Hell's Kitchen
* Astoria
* Lower Manhattan

**Key finding:**
Hell's Kitchen generated the highest revenue, although all three locations performed relatively closely.

**Business opportunity:**
The company can investigate what makes the highest-performing store successful and apply those practices across other locations.

---

### 3. Customer Purchasing Behaviour by Time of Day

Transactions were grouped into:

* Morning
* Afternoon
* Evening
* Night

**Key finding:**
Morning is the strongest sales period by a significant margin, showing that Bright Coffee's business is heavily driven by morning customer demand.

**Business opportunity:**

* Prioritise staffing during peak morning hours
* Improve service speed
* Offer breakfast bundles
* Promote coffee and bakery combinations

At the same time, the business should explore ways to strengthen afternoon sales and evaluate whether very late operating hours are financially worthwhile.

---

### 4. Product Category Performance

The analysis compared sales across product categories.

**Key finding:**
Coffee and Tea are the strongest revenue-generating categories, while Bakery also provides a valuable opportunity for increasing the average value of each transaction.

**Business opportunity:**
Bright Coffee can use product bundling strategies such as:

* Coffee + pastry
* Tea + bakery item
* Morning breakfast combinations
* Premium drink + snack bundles

---

### 5. Best-Performing Products

Individual product types were analysed to identify the products that customers purchase most successfully.

Some of the strongest products included:

* Barista Espresso
* Brewed Chai Tea
* Hot Chocolate
* Gourmet Brewed Coffee

**Business opportunity:**
These products should remain highly visible, consistently available, and central to promotional strategies.

Bright Coffee can also use these popular products as opportunities to recommend complementary bakery items or premium upgrades.

---

### 6. Sales by Day of the Week

Daily sales performance was analysed to identify stronger and weaker trading days.

**Key finding:**
Sales remain relatively consistent across the week, with some variation between weekdays and weekends.

**Business opportunity:**
Bright Coffee can use targeted promotions on weaker days and investigate whether customer purchasing behaviour changes during weekends.

---

### 7. Store and Product Category Performance

A Store × Product Category analysis was created to understand what drives revenue at each location.

**Business opportunity:**
Different locations may have different customer preferences. This creates an opportunity for Bright Coffee to use **location-specific promotions rather than applying the same strategy across every store**.

# 💡 Strategic Recommendations

Based on the analysis, Bright Coffee can drive future growth by focusing on the following areas.

## 1. Increase Morning Efficiency

Morning sales are the most important revenue driver.

Bright Coffee should:

* Prepare high-volume products in advance
* Introduce express ordering where possible
* Reduce waiting times during peak hours

---

## 2. Introduce Coffee and Bakery Bundles

Coffee and Bakery are natural complementary products.

Potential offers could include:

* Coffee + muffin
* Espresso + pastry
* Breakfast coffee bundle
* Tea + bakery afternoon special

---

## 3. Grow Afternoon Sales

The business currently relies heavily on morning demand.

Bright Coffee could test:

* Afternoon promotions
* Iced coffee offers
* Coffee and dessert combinations
* Loyalty rewards during slower periods
