☕ Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

This project analyses Bright Coffee’s total revenue to understand what is driving sales and where the business can improve.

Key findings

June is the strongest revenue month, while January and February are the weakest.

Daily revenue is fairly consistent, with no major dependence on a particular day.

Revenue is evenly split among the three stores, with each contributing roughly one-third of the total.

Astoria performs better at night, suggesting a potential evening sales opportunity.

Coffee is the biggest revenue category, followed by Tea.

Revenue is concentrated among a few high-performing products, particularly Barista Espresso, Brewed Chai Tea, Gourmet Beans, and House Blend.

Review weaker products before deciding whether to promote, reposition, or remove them.

Main recommendations

Protect best-selling products with strong stock levels and menu visibility.
Grow Coffee and Tea sales, as they are the strongest revenue categories.
Use promotions during weaker months, particularly January and February.
Create product bundles to increase the amount customers spend per transaction.
Use store-specific strategies, especially around Astoria's stronger evening performance.

Overall message

Bright Coffee's biggest revenue opportunities are in its product mix and monthly performance. The business should protect its strongest products, improve weaker periods, and use targeted promotions and bundles to increase revenue.

The project is designed to move beyond simply reporting sales and help management make practical revenue decisions.
